About Western Maryland Resource Hub

Our Mission

The Western Maryland Resource Hub is dedicated to connecting individuals and families in Allegany, Garrett, and Washington counties with essential services and support. We believe everyone deserves access to quality resources regardless of their circumstances.

What We Do

We maintain a comprehensive, regularly updated database of local, state, and national resources covering everything from food assistance and housing to mental health services, employment support, and specialized programs for veterans, seniors, and other vulnerable populations.

Our Commitment

We are committed to:

  • ✓ Keeping resource information accurate and current
  • ✓ Making our database accessible to all users
  • ✓ Serving all counties in Western Maryland equitably
  • ✓ Protecting user privacy and data security
  • ✓ Continuously expanding our resource coverage

Get Involved

Know of a resource we're missing? Have suggestions for improvement? We'd love to hear from you! You can submit new resources directly through our platform or contact us with feedback.
