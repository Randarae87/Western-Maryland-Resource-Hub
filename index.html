About Western Maryland Resource Hub

The Western Maryland Resource Hub connects residents of Allegany, Garrett, and Washington Counties with vital community services and support programs.

Our mission is to make it easy for individuals and families to find help when they need it most — from food assistance and housing to healthcare, employment, and crisis support.

How to Use This Hub

  • Use the search bar to find resources by keyword
  • Filter by category using the buttons above
  • Click "Call" to contact organizations directly
  • Visit websites or send emails for more information
  • Submit new resources using the "Add Resource" button

Our Service Areas

  • Allegany County
  • Garrett County
  • Washington County
